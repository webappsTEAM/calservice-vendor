# SEVO — Day-One Launch Film
### "One App. Every Day." — 75-Second Hero Script (Product-as-Hero)

**Format:** 16:9 master (cut down to 9:16/1:1 later if needed)
**Style reference:** Apple product-launch films (e.g. iPhone/Apple Watch reveal spots) — clean studio backgrounds, macro UI shots, confident negative space, typography that does the talking. The product and its interface are the actors; people appear only as hands and quiet context, never as a "customer testimonial."
**Voice:** No narrator track. Sound design + music + on-screen type carry the story. (An optional single-line VO for the open and close is included at the bottom if you'd rather have a voice anchor.)

---

## Visual language (set once, reuse everywhere)

- **Background:** Seamless white or deep charcoal studio sweep — never a real street/home, so the app UI stays the highest-contrast thing in frame.
- **Palette:** One accent colour carries the whole film — recommend the cream/teal pairing already locked for the SEVO deck, desaturated everywhere except the UI itself, which stays full colour. That contrast is what makes the product "pop" the way Apple's product shots do.
- **Typography:** A single grotesque/sans family (System/SF-style or your existing brand face), set small and precise — never a "marketing" script font.
- **Camera:** Locked-off or ultra-slow push-ins only. No handheld, no whip pans. Cuts are hard and on the beat, not dissolves.
- **Motion graphics:** Real screen recordings of the app (not mockup renders) — browsing, tapping, cart, checkout, tracking. Bezel-free, floating in space, casting a soft shadow.

## Optional minimal-VO version

If you want a single human voice as a bookend (Apple does this occasionally — one calm line at open, one at close, nothing in between):

- **At 0:04** (as the phone rises): *"There's a lot that happens in a day."*
- **At 0:58** (on the logo reform): *"SEVO. One app. Every day."*

Nothing else is spoken — the middle stays 100% visual, exactly as scripted above.

---

## Production notes

- **Shoot list:** phone product shots (5 setups: logo/idle, home-screen, booking flow, tracking, cart/checkout), 3 tabletop macro setups (vegetables, parcel/moving box, delivery bag), all on the same seamless background for consistency.
- **Screen-record early:** capture the actual app footage for booking, tracking, and cart in the first session — the whole film is paced around real UI motion, so placeholder screens will throw off every cut timing downstream.
- **Music:** a single instrumental track that builds in three visible layers (matches the three chapters) and resolves at the close — brief the composer/library search around "restrained," "confident," "one melodic idea," not "energetic" or "corporate upbeat."
- **What to avoid:** no customer or vendor testimonials, no voiceover list of features, no crowded multi-shot montages of "happy people" — the whole point of product-as-hero is that the interface and the object carry the emotion.
